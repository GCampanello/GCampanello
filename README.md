## Projects

**Expected value and risk in 10eLotto.** The exact payout distribution of all 60 possible bets of an Italian keno game, checked with Monte Carlo simulations, plus statistical tests on 768 real draws. No bet has a positive expected value: the best one returns 71.1% of the stake. [Paper (PDF, in Italian)](https://gcampanello.github.io/papers/10eLotto_Campanello.pdf)

**Take-home pay and long-run wealth under Italian tax rules.** A model of an employee's pay in Milan under the 2026 rules: the effective marginal rate, the fiscal drag from brackets that do not follow inflation, and a Monte Carlo of savings over 30 years.
